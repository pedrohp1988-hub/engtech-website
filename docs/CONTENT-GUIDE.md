# Content Guide

## Voice

Clear, capable, direct, and technically grounded. Prefer specific outcomes over broad claims. Avoid unexplained acronyms and unsupported superlatives.

## Page requirements

Each service or city page should include a unique title and description, canonical URL, customer problem, delivery approach, proof, FAQs, service area details, and a clear contact path.

## SEO quality bar

Content must be useful to facility decision-makers and materially unique. City pages should reflect real coverage, regional equipment needs, response capabilities, and applicable regulatory context.
